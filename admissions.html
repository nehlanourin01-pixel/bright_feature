FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers to queries regarding applications, eligibility, and scholarship programs.

What are the criteria for the Merit-based Scholarship?

Our academic advisors evaluate your scholarship eligibility based on three factors: previous academic achievement, details provided in your Statement of Purpose (SOP), and a short, 20-minute basic diagnostic aptitude evaluation conducted online during step 3 of the counseling session.

Can I attend classes on a part-time basis or during weekends?

Yes, absolutely! We understand that many students work full-time or have ongoing commitments. We offer dedicated evening batches (7:00 PM - 9:30 PM, Mon-Fri) as well as full intensive Weekend-only cohorts (9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Saturday & Sunday).

Is the placement support guarantee backed by partnerships?

Yes, we have 150+ direct corporate placement partners across major IT hubs. While we provide 100% placement support—including resume crafting, direct mock reviews, and scheduling referral interview slots—the ultimate conversion depends entirely on the student's performance.

Are the online cohort sessions recorded if I miss a live class?

Yes, every live remote session is immediately recorded in high-definition and uploaded to your personal student portal dashboard within 2 hours of the class. You will maintain lifetime, unrestricted access to all dashboard video logs and class repositories.
